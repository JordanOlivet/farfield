### Done

- **Streaming across loaders.** A Fabric client on a Forge server got no
  answer (`Received invalid discriminator byte 33 on channel
  farfield:stream`): Forge's `SimpleChannel` puts a discriminator byte
  before the payload. The Forge side is now a raw channel
  (`EventNetworkChannel`) carrying Fabric's bytes. The handshake accepts
  any version of the other side; the stream protocol's hello declines
  another version, as ADR-070 decided (no new decision). The other way, a
  Forge client on a Fabric server built its own LODs: Forge 47 keeps only
  the channel names followed by a zero byte, and Fabric ends its
  `minecraft:register` list without one, so Farfield's channel, last of
  the list, was dropped. `ClientPacketListenerMixin` (forge project) reads
  the list beside Forge.
- **Sodium 0.5 and Embeddium 0.3 (plan S.3).** No mixin of ours is needed:
  the Sodium 0.8 one is an optional shortcut and its plugin leaves it out.
  The hand-off rule was wrong on the rim: Sodium 0.5.13 and Embeddium
  0.3.31 test a section's distance on its own box (read in both jars),
  Sodium 0.8.13 grows it by one block. The LODs hid their water where
  Embeddium drew nothing: dark pits over the sea at the far edge of the
  game's view. `u_sodiumGrow` (0 for the `me.jellysquid` Sodium, 1
  otherwise), and the log says which.
- **Shader packs (plan S.1, S.2).** The nine Iris targets, read in Iris
  1.7.6 and in Oculus 1.8.0: eight are the same as in Iris 1.8. The ninth,
  `ShaderPack`'s constructor in `Iris.loadExternalShaderpack`, has no "is a
  zip" flag there, so the contract macro was never added and the renderer
  said `LOD rendering off under shader pack ...: the pack was loaded
  without a LOD contract's macro`. A second optional injection takes the
  older call. On Forge the renderer also looked for the mod `iris`: Oculus
  has Iris's classes under the id `oculus`.
- **Tools.** `dev/rehearsal/prod-client.sh` starts a production client
  (launcher layout, shipped jars) through portablemc with an offline name,
  installs kept in `runs/pmc` (gitignored, 0.8 GB), and runs a Rehearsal
  command file in it; `dev/rehearsal/fetch-mods.py` downloads test mods
  from Modrinth, each checked against its sha512. Rehearsal's own scripts
  use portablemc the same way for NeoForge packs.

- **The two Rehearsal points of the night.** The pack launchers took the
  newest jar by date, whatever its Minecraft version:
  `dev/rehearsal/fabric-pack.py` here, `fabric_pack.py` and
  `neoforge_pack.py` in Rehearsal. Each now reads the instance's Minecraft
  version and takes that version's jar. Rehearsal's `close-game.ps1` looked
  for the run dir on the JVM's command line, which a dev run does not carry
  (`--gameDir .`): it also reads the JVM's working directory.

### The user's shader pack test: a black chunk (afternoon)

The user turned Complementary Reimagined on in the same pack: "je viens de
voir un chunk noir", three screenshots at 208 117 -128. A dark square, one
chunk wide, on the shore of a lake about 125 blocks away, still there 48 s
later.

- **Cause.** The renderer leaves a LOD section out when the game draws
  all of it (`gameDrawsAll`, ADR-047). That test still grew each chunk
  section by one block, Sodium 0.8's rule, while the shader rule follows
  Sodium 0.5 since `a01a7d2`. Grown, a section's diagonal corner comes up
  to 1.4 blocks nearer: a chunk just past the render distance (128.3
  blocks there, for a limit of 128) counted as drawn by the game. Sodium
  did not draw it, and its LOD was skipped: nothing in that chunk. It
  takes a level-0 section on the rim, so a large window (3840x2019 there);
  at 854x480 the rim is level 1 and the case does not show.
- **Fix.** `nearest` takes the same growth as the shader (`sodiumGrow`),
  in the skip test and in the `debug here` report.
- **Rehearsal.** The pack did not start with Rehearsal: its 1.20.1 hook
  on the game's constructor was a redirect whose `at` is a list in the
  Fabric jar, which the pack's MixinExtras 0.5.4 fails on. Replaced by an
  argument change (Rehearsal `6f1b1ed`).

Verified, in the user's pack run in production from a folder of its own
(`runs/prominence`, the instance only read, a copy of his save, Rehearsal,
3840x2019, the time of day fixed, his position and view):

- The shipped jar: the dark square is there, as on his screenshots; with
  `farfield render off` the game's terrain stops before that chunk.
- The fixed jar, same view: the chunk shows its LOD; near-black pixels in
  the crop around it fall from 1,913 to 129, and the frame without LODs
  is unchanged (39 pixels of 80,000).
- `build` with every version and 1.20.1 after the fix: green, 12 jars.
- A Forge 1.20.1 dev client with the rebuilt Rehearsal: no injection
  error, `Rehearsal window: minimized at launch`, quit answered.

Not verified: why the same view after `shader off` in that run showed no
hole with the shipped jar (the renderer reloads at the switch, which may
leave that chunk uncompiled; not measured). Two tries in the small test
client (854x480, Sodium 0.5.13) showed no difference before and after the
fix, for the level reason above. The user's own retest with the fixed jar.
