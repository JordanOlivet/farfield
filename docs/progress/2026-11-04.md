## What is established

- Not a mod, not the shader path: the replay without any shader pack shows
  the same squares, black. The no-shader run of 2026-11-03 was clean by
  chance (about 1 run in 3 shows them on a fresh world).
- They are on the noise preview's sections (gone with `preview off`), in
  the LOD translucent pass's area, and they follow the run's data: run 9's
  saved world reproduces them every time now, at the same place across the
  last four replays.
- `customId` is ruled out. Biome blending looked involved here; the
  in-run A/B below showed the blend-0 replay was run-to-run variance.

## Then: the squares are holes in the preview's water (2 no-shader replays)

- Without a shader the whole sea floor is black (light fades with depth);
  the squares show that floor: water missing, not dark water.
  `debug levels` put them on the preview's level 1 in that run.
- Ruled out by counters added for it: water tops culled by the section
  above (only water under water, as designed), water tops missing from
  the meshes (54 level-1 meshes checked, all complete). A forced biome
  blend (0, 1, 3, 7 in one run) changed nothing: the earlier blend-0 replay
  was run-to-run variance. The squares, projected on the sea plane and
  compared with the region files, lie on preview chunks, away from real
  data. An offline probe of the run 9 world's preview tiles found sound
  data in the dark cells (water to the top layer, `ocean`, `deep_ocean`).

## The cause

`GlBuffer` made every store with `glNamedBufferStorage` and no data, whose
contents are undefined. `previewHidden` (binding 8, the chunk columns where
the preview gives way to real terrain, ADR-100) is uploaded only at the
first hand-over, and the translucent pass of every preview section tests it
(`lod.vert` sets handoff bit 2 from the section's preview flag). Until a
hand-over, the shader read the driver's leftovers: wherever a bit was set,
the preview's water was discarded. The opaque floor stayed, since solid
sections draw with the `SOLID` variant, which never tests the grid. It fits
every observation: random per run, stable within one, through a resource
reload and `preview off`/`on`, with or without a pack, chunk-column
rectangles, more often on a replayed world (fewer hand-overs).

Fix: `GlBuffer.create` clears each new store to zeros
(`glClearNamedBufferData`, `GL_R8UI`, null data), which `grow` uses too.
The diagnostic counters were removed again (`LodRenderer` back to its
state before `2168d22`); the agent `reload` verb and the launcher switches
stay.
