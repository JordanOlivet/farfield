## What is established

- Not a mod, not the shader path: the replay without any shader pack shows
  the same squares, black. The no-shader run of 2026-11-03 was clean by
  chance (about 1 run in 3 shows them on a fresh world).
- They are on the noise preview's sections (gone with `preview off`), in
  the LOD translucent pass's area, and they follow the run's data: run 9's
  saved world reproduces them every time now, at the same place across the
  last four replays.
- `customId` is ruled out; biome blending plays a part, not the whole.
