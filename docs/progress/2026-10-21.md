## Preview meshing (follow-up of E.16, ADR-048)

The user tested the Fabric client while this ran offline, then freed it.
A JMH benchmark without corpus (`PreviewBenchmark`: a synthetic tile of
rolling land and sea, 10 spruces per chunk) splits a preview mesh job,
single thread, us per operation:

| | Level 0 | Level 2 | Level 4 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Whole section, forest | 60 | 334 | 1,361 |
| Whole section, no trees | 58 | 137 | 120 |
| Six neighbor layers | 149 | 178 | 196 |
| Mesher | 50 | 261 | 266 |

Trees were 92 % of a level-4 section: some 11,000 trees stamped block by
block. On coarse levels the stamp now adds areas (the trunk as a vertical
run, each crown row as its square cut by the voxel grid, less rounded
corners and the trunk), block by block only on level 0, in layers, or
when a crown reaches a real chunk. Whole section with forest: level 2 334
to 254 us, level 4 1,361 to 718 us. `coarseTreesByAreaMatchBlockByBlock`
compares both ways on levels 1 to 4 (every crown shape, a real chunk).

In game (Fabric dev client, 4 mesher threads), after a 2,500-block
teleport into preview terrain: 0.9 to 1.6 ms per job, as the benchmark
predicts; 15,554 jobs in 30 s took about 25 s of the 120 thread-seconds
there were. Meshing is no longer the limit after a teleport: the 64 jobs
in flight and the upload budget per frame are. The earlier 2.5 to 3.7 ms
per job were measured while the preview tiles were being built on 4 more
threads. Image correct after the teleport.

Not verified: NeoForge, the user's pack.

## Snow on preview tree tops (E.11, ADR-049)

The first next step of the morning. A tree kind now carries a snow layer
when the block above its crown is cold enough to snow (vanilla's
freeze_top_layer, the chunk's biome); `PreviewVoxels` lays it over each
crown block no row above covers, after every tree, and shows it on top
of coarse tree voxels. Tile format 6.

Four Fabric dev client runs at the snowy taiga east of x 2900 (render
distance 2), where real terrain from earlier runs meets the preview;
`/farfield preview off` told the two apart:

1. Snow present, but preview trees looked like white wedding cakes next
   to real spruces (green sides, white rings).
2. Spruces given vanilla's tiers (`TIERED`): no change at that spot. A
   dump of the stored tile showed the culprit, the pine kind: a cone of
   radius 4 over 5 rows (vanilla's pine widens one block per row), so
   capped at its rows.
3. Pines right, but rows were full squares: white frames. Vanilla's
   spruce and pine leave out row corners from radius 1.
4. Corners left out: preview trees round with green sides, close to the
   real ones beside them, a little whiter. Night shot: same light on both.

Also fixed: spruce and pine placers were found by class name, which is
an intermediary name on Fabric outside the dev client, so they drew as
blobs there; now `instanceof`, the name kept for modded placers.

`PreviewBenchmark` (whole section, now `none`, `bare`, `snowy` spruces):
level 2 232 us bare, 266 snowy; level 4 630 and 730; level 0 55 both.
Unit tests: snow per column, snow never over another tree's leaves,
tiers and their snow, columns without snow, coarse snow from areas equal
to block by block. Not verified: NeoForge, the user's pack.
