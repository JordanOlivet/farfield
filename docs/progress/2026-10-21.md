## Holes in far snowy forests (ADR-050)

The user, Fabric dev client right after `6ac8998`, with a spyglass: "j'ai
vu des trucs bizarres, comme si les feuilles étaient transparentes"
(three screenshots in the run dir). Reproduced at `/tp 2700 170 -100 -90
25` (render distance 8): the preview forest by the mountain, at level 1
(level colors), showed holes down to the ice even with level colors on.

Cause: on coarse levels a snow layer voxel is drawn as a full cube, but
its side textures held 2 of 16 rows, the rest cutout, so the sides were
cut away while the voxel hid its neighbors. Fixed in `MaterialColors`
(partial blocks' faces filled with their own color; real coarse voxels
with a snow layer had the same holes). With the holes gone the far
forest turned to white cubes: coarse preview snow now follows a vote
(snow layers over half the voxel's columns), and the forest shows green
and white like the real trees beside it. Captures before and after at
the same spot; 21 preview tests pass, the coarse snow test now checks
the vote.

Not verified: NeoForge, the user's pack, real slabs at coarse levels
(their sides now filled instead of cut).

## Hollow preview trees (ADR-051)

The user, after `8bd49b9`, with a spyglass from 2700, 170, -100: "il en
reste encore" (six screenshots). Rings of leaves with floating snow,
seen through to the ice. Up close at render distance 2 (spectator, then
creative on a glass block for the spyglass, removed afterwards): in a
preview pine every leaf in a horizontal layer holding snow layers was
missing. An offline scan of the stored tiles (temporary core tests, not
kept) found the data and the level-0 meshes complete, so the renderer:
`LodRenderer.mesh` took the classifier from the key's level, which has
an offset for preview keys, and meshed level-0 preview sections with the
coarse classifier (snow layer as an opaque cube). Now the draw level.
After the fix the same pine is whole, and the spyglass view from the
user's spot shows full crowns. The depth pre-pass was ruled out on the
way (same image with `/farfield debug prepass off`).

Not verified: NeoForge, the user's pack.
