## Shapes on LOD mountains (`1084a20`)

- The user highlighted the shapes in red on a capture: straight lines on the
  section borders (32 blocks) and a curve. `lod.frag` counted voxels outside
  the section as open, so every section border was lighter, and occlusion
  stopped past level 1 (a ring). Each section slot now carries the slots of
  its 26 resident same-level neighbors (kept in sync on upload and
  eviction); occlusion reads across borders on every level.
- Checked: the before/after difference image of a steep mountain falls
  exactly on the section borders.

## Flicker at the vanilla edge

- The user's video, frame 57 (5.37 s): a band of chunks shows nothing for
  one frame at the vanilla edge. Reproduced in the dev client with Sodium
  by flying forward (key held, 40 captures): a dark gray square for one
  capture where neither the game nor the LODs drew.
- Cause: a section the game finishes building is compiled at once, but it
  enters Sodium's render lists on the next frame; LODs gave way the frame
  it was compiled. The compiled-sections grid now keeps the raw state of
  the last pass and LODs give way once a section has been compiled for two
  passes; the outer ring of the grid (3 chunks) is read every frame, the
  rest every 8 frames or when the grid moves (its state is remapped).
- Checked with a detector (`holes.py` in the scratchpad: dark gray 16-px
  blocks present in one capture and absent in both neighbors), validated on
  the old flight (it finds the one hole, no false positive): 0 holes in 3
  flights of 40 captures with the fix, against 1 in 40 before. 0.70 ms
  render thread, 3.9 ms GPU at 4K.
- Not verified: the NeoForge dev client (same common code), the user's pack.
