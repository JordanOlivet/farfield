## Cheaper plants' pass

The user's pack with `/farfield debug plants`: savanna "460 fps" off
against "380 fps" on (plants 385 to 466 us for 119k to 142k plant quads),
kelp 360 against 325. The user: "go 1+2". The plants' pass has its own
program (`#define PLANTS`): holes cut before any shading, the tint blended
once per vertex at the plant's column (`biome_tint.glsl`, shared by both
stages through a `#include` line that `GlProgram` inlines). A first try
put that code in the shared shaders: plants -35 us but opaque +126 us,
so it moved to the variant. Fabric dev client, plains at (-642, 110, 594),
old and new shaders alternated (comparable runs: pre-pass 680 us):

| Shaders | Plants us | Opaque us | Translucent us | GPU us |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Old | 249 | 1,183 | 563 | 2,676 |
| New (1st) | 219 | 1,186 | 564 | 2,653 |
| New (2nd) | 219 | 1,180 | 563 | 2,643 |

Plants -12 %, the rest unchanged: a small gain. The user's pack after
it: "Je suis à 425 fps contre 450 fps environ, c'est acceptable". The first run of each
series ran slower in every pass (pre-pass 735 us against 680): compare
runs with the same pre-pass time.
