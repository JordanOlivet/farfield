## Plants on the finest level (ADR-059)

The user, after the preview caches: "il faudra aussi que sur la preview,
on rajoute tout ce qui est herbe etc... Actuellement, les plaines sont
affreusement vides", then "attaque l'herbe et les fleurs". Every LOD left
plants without a collision out (`BlockKeys`), real terrain too. Of three
options (crossed quads plus a far tint, a tint only, low boxes), the
user chose crossed quads plus a tint.

Done, step 1 of 3: plants as crossed quads on the finest level of real
LODs (class `PLANT`, diagonals 6 and 7, own group and draw list). No
import: level-0 sections already hold the plants.

Fabric dev client, a server-side bench (local, not committed) finding a
dry plains from the generator (-1154, 882) and looking at it from 110
blocks west after generating it:

| Run | GPU per frame | Quads in view |
|---|---|---|
| Plants left out | 2,255 us | 2,040,692 |
| Plants in their sections' passes | 3,688 us | 2,088,636 |
| Plants in their own pass | 2,468 us | 2,088,645 |

Images: the LOD band beyond vanilla's grass shows tufts and flowers where
it was flat green; the same look with both plant draws. A first bench
looked at a lake (the 3D biome search returned a point over water) and
was dropped.
