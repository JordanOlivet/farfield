## Coarse levels and preview plants (ADR-060)

The user, after step 1 in the Fabric pack: "J'ai testé, c'est nikel,
attaque le reste".

- Covered grounds: `CoveredKey`, the mip rule (`MIPS_VERSION` 3), the
  speckled top in `MaterialColors`.
- Preview: tile format 8 with per-chunk plants; `PreviewVoxels` places
  them on level 0 by block hash and covers the ground on coarser levels.
- Tests: `plantsOnTheGroundCoverItInQuarters` (half and full cover, a
  plant in the brick above, quarters passed up a level),
  `plantsGrowOnTheirGroundAtTheChunksDensity` (about 63 % at 256 per

  open (8,858 sections), `minecraft:plains grows 0.10 trees per chunk of
  3 kinds, 216.1 plants of 5 kinds`, tufts and flowers over the preview to
