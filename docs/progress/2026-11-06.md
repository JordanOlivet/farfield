## Fabric pack: LOD cost under shader packs (user's run, 17:53-17:56)

The user reported 100 fps without Farfield against 60-70 fps with it, under
every shader pack (Cobblemon instance, 4K 3840x2019, RTX 4070 Ti SUPER,
vanilla render distance 4, `voxelPixels=6`). Performance logs, about 1500
sections and 2 M quads sent per frame:

| Pack | LOD GPU per frame | Render thread |
|---|---|---|
| none | ~2.0 ms | ~0.8 ms |
| BSL | 2.0-3.0 ms | ~1.0 ms |
| Complementary Reimagined | ~3.2 ms | ~0.9 ms |
| MakeUp UltraFast | 2.9-3.2 ms | ~0.9 ms |
| Photon (gcea) | 2.0-2.6 ms | ~0.8 ms |

- The fps drop is about 5.4 ms per frame; our measured passes are 2 to
  3.3 ms of it, the rest is presumably the pack's deferred passes over LOD
  pixels that were sky before (not measured).
- The translucent pass reads 0 us under a shader pack: its time is either
  not timed on that path or not drawn there. Unverified.
- Depth pre-pass 0.7-1 ms per frame, plants 0.2-0.6 ms.
- The user's check: `/farfield detail 10` instead of 6 raises the fps
  ("oui ça remonte"), so quad count is a lever. Numbers not reported.
- The client then crashed on a native OutOfMemoryError in Sodium's chunk
  builder: Windows commit limit exhausted (AvailPageFile 6 MB of 49 GB, game
  9.6 GB). Not a Farfield allocation; the user's high commit charge is to be
  looked into later.

## Translucent LOD overdraw under a shader pack (agent run, 18:58-19:03)

A temporary probe (never committed, reverted, jar rebuilt without it):
`GL_FRAGMENT_SHADER_INVOCATIONS` and `GL_SAMPLES_PASSED` around
`drawPackTranslucent`, and every 10 s a readback of both LOD depths to count
the pixels where LOD water lies in front of the opaque LODs. Fabric pack in
production, Complementary Reimagined r5.9.3, 854x480, render distance 4.
Steady values per frame:

| Scene | Fragments shaded | Passed depth | Visible LOD water pixels |
|---|---|---|---|
| A sea from above, 1808 140 1700, pitch 25 | 256k | 253k | 253k |
| B sea horizon, 1808 80 1700, pitch 3 | 204k | 59k | 59k |
| C spawn ice, 48 120 32, pitch 5 | 233k | 185k | 98k |
| D icebergs, 2394 126 -162 | 232k | 123k | 110k |

- Open sea (A, B): one shaded fragment per visible water pixel. The extra
  fragments of B are the near water that vanilla draws (lower half of the
  capture, render distance 4): `lod.frag` discards them on its first line,
  on `u_gameDepth`, before the pack's code.
- Frozen water (C): 1.9 layers per visible pixel, ice over water.
- So a translucent depth pre-pass that keeps the look (option 1, early
  rejection behind LOD terrain) would save next to nothing on these views;
  one fragment per pixel (option 2) would only help frozen water, and loses
  the water under the ice. The translucent cost under a pack is the pack's
  water shader once per pixel, as Voxy pays it. Not pursued.
- GPU times of that run are not usable: the probe read its queries at once
  and stalled the frame.

## Opaque pass under a shader pack: biome blend (agent runs, 19:15-19:20)

Fabric pack in production, Complementary Reimagined r5.9.3, 1920x1080
(`overrideWidth`/`overrideHeight` in `fabric/runs/prod/options.txt`, put
back after), plains at -1264, 107, 882 facing east, pitch 10, one run per
value of vanilla's Biome Blend (`biomeBlendRadius`; the user's pack has 7,
the maximum). Steady stat lines, about 400 sections, 380k quads sent:

| Biome blend | LOD GPU | Pre-pass | Opaque | Plants | Translucent |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 7 | 761 us | 152 | 421 | 62 | 125 |
| 0 | 601 us | 152 | 291 | 53 | 104 |

- Blend 7 costs 31 % of the opaque pass and 17 % of the translucent one
  here, 21 % of the LOD GPU time. `blendedTint` reads up to 5x5 columns
  per tinted fragment at level 0, every frame; vanilla blends once per
  vertex when it builds a chunk.
- Lever: blend once per 4x4 column and tint kind when a section (or a
  neighbor it reads) is uploaded, and let the fragment read 4 of them
  (bilinear) instead of up to 25. Not started.
