- The occlusion costs 57 us of the opaque pass under Complementary (+20 %),
  36 us without a pack (+19 %): 8 to 10 % of the LOD GPU time at 1080p. By
  pixel count about 0.15 to 0.23 ms at 4K. For scale, the biome blend per
  column (ADR-115) saved 120 to 145 us at the same spot.
- Visible: with it off, the mid-distance hillsides lose their shading
  between steps (captures `ao-on-1` and `ao-off-1` of the first run).
- A first run on a fresh world was still loading (234 to 474 sections
  during the cycles); its deltas agree (+62 to 66 us opaque).
- Not changed: the occlusion keeps its per-fragment reads. A per-vertex or
  per-quad version would need the mesher to split greedy quads where the
  occlusion changes (more quads).
- Found: the LOD occlusion ignores vanilla's Smooth Lighting option
  (`ao` in `options.txt`); it is always on.
