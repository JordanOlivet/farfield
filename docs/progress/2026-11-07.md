Fabric pack in production (`dev/agent/fabric-prod-client.py`), 1920x1080
(`overrideWidth`/`overrideHeight` in `fabric/runs/prod/options.txt`, put
back after), Biome Blend 7, uncapped fps, render distance 4, plains at

Three off/on cycles of 22 s after a 120 s settle; steady scene, 483

| Shader pack | AO | LOD GPU | Pre-pass | Opaque | Translucent |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Complementary Reimagined r5.9.3 | on | 691 us | 170 | 345 | 122 |
| Complementary Reimagined r5.9.3 | off | 635 us | 172 | 288 | 118 |
| none | on | 394 us | 135 | 227 | 8 |
| none | off | 359 us | 136 | 191 | 8 |

- The occlusion costs 57 us of the opaque pass under Complementary (+20 %),
  36 us without a pack (+19 %): 8 to 10 % of the LOD GPU time at 1080p. By
  pixel count about 0.15 to 0.23 ms at 4K. For scale, the biome blend per
  column (ADR-115) saved 120 to 145 us at the same spot.
- Visible: with it off, the mid-distance hillsides lose their shading
  between steps (captures `ao-on-1` and `ao-off-1` of the first run).
- A first run on a fresh world was still loading (234 to 474 sections
  during the cycles); its deltas agree (+62 to 66 us opaque).
- Not changed: the occlusion keeps its per-fragment reads. A per-vertex or
  per-quad version would need the mesher to split greedy quads where the
  occlusion changes (more quads).
- Found: the LOD occlusion ignores vanilla's Smooth Lighting option
  (`ao` in `options.txt`); it is always on.

- C.1d / D.6 (the user's pick: light plus the sources kept in the tiles,
  ADR-117). A probe of the 35 stored tiles of the test world found no light
  source in the feature runs (only amethyst blocks, which do not emit), so
  the light alone would have shown nothing: sea pickles were stored as the
  sea's water, torches have no mesh class, and a single source never fills
  a 2-block feature voxel. Now `PreviewFeatures` keeps every block with a
  light emission as a light source of the tile (format 17, stream protocol
  8), `PreviewVoxels` lights the voxels around each one when the client
  builds a section, and the client setting `preview_light` (settings
  screen, `/farfield preview light on|off`, default on) turns it off; a
  switch meshes the resident preview sections again (logged). The minute
  line counts the light sources.
