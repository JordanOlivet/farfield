## Shaped blocks (ADR-036, plan item E.10)

Next step 2 of 2026-10-17: fences, walls and panes were invisible in LODs
and stairs were full cubes.

- `MaterialClassifier.SHAPED` and `ShapeBoxes` (core): a block's outline
  boxes in sixteenths, at most 8, kept per block state in `MaterialTable`
  (`Resolver.boxes`). `BlockKeys` classes as shaped the non-full blocks
  that cover their footprint without standing on the bottom (stairs, top
  slabs) and those with a collision shape (fences, walls, panes, bars,
  doors); blocks without collision (plants, torches, rails) stay out.
- `BinaryGreedyMesher` meshes each box of a shaped voxel as unit quads,
  box index in the fill bits; faces on the voxel side are culled by full
  opaque neighbors and lit by the voxel in front, inner faces by the
  shaped voxel. Coarse levels treat shaped blocks as opaque (`LodRenderer`).
- `MaterialColors` entries grow to 27 ints with the boxes; `lod.vert`
  moves the corners to the box; `lod.frag` discards textured texels below
  half coverage in the opaque pass (pane and bar frames, vanilla cutout).
- Tests: `shapedVoxelsMeshTheirBoxes`, `MaterialTableTest` (2); core 93
  tests green; `gradlew build` green.

## Preview flicker and holes (fix)

The user's pass in the Fabric pack with ADR-039: "C'est beaucoup mieux (il
manque encore les arbres à minima)", but areas next to explored land that
never showed up despite minutes of waiting, and whole areas flickering
while only turning the camera (video `flicker-2.mp4`, frames 51, 262 and
625). Frames extracted to `Desktop/flicker-2-frames` with their numbers.

- The three frames: for one frame, the same rectangle of preview terrain
  behind a hill shows the sky. Cause: every real level-4 write dropped the
  mask of its tile, and a tile without a mask leaves the selection until
  the mask is read again (a frame); the server rewrites real sections
  around the player even at rest, and constantly while exploring, so
  tiles next to explored land flickered or stayed missing.
- Fix (`PreviewLayer`): a write marks the tile's mask dirty and reads it
  again in the background while the old one stays in use; only a mask
  that changed meshes the tile again. The user's log: tiles built in 2.6 s
  each, no failure, no Farfield error.
- Fabric dev client at 6000, 0: four `/fill` of 10,571 blocks next to the
  preview, 16 captures during the writes, none with sky below the
  horizon; no error in the log. The one-frame hole was not reproduced
  before the fix in the dev client, so this check alone does not prove it.

## Preview gap beside real water (fix)

The user's next pass: no more flicker, but areas still "not loaded" at the
border with real terrain (screenshot at 182, 160, -403): where the real
LODs or vanilla show water the preview did not foresee, everything under
the preview's first layer is empty and the sky shows through the real
water. The user's idea: fill under the preview next to real water. Agreed,
with two parts:

- The preview's skin was computed from preview heights only: a column
  beside a real sea stopped one voxel under its preview neighbors, far
  above the real sea floor. Now a real neighbor counts with its real
  ground: the column goes down to the real floor (`PreviewVoxels`).
- The mask material of real columns rose to the preview's height, so it
  also hid the preview land's side facing real water. Real columns now
  hold an opaque mask up to the real ground and a translucent mask
  (`farfield:preview_mask_fluid`, class translucent in `BlockKeys`) up to
  the real fluid's top: preview water still shows no wall toward real
  water, preview land shows its cliff. Real ground and fluid top per chunk
  come from the real level-4 voxels and their fill, read with the mask
  (`PreviewLayer`); the selection lets preview sections reach down to the
  real ground of the chunks around (`Summary.spans` with a lowest block).
- Tests: `columnsBesideARealSeaGoDownToItsFloor`, the mask test rewritten
  for the two materials; core 103 tests green, build green.
- Fabric dev client: no error in the log, preview drawn as before. The
  exact case (preview land beside real deep water) was not reproduced: a
  pit dug at the edge of the loaded chunks lay in open sea the first time,
  and beside real forest the second time (the loaded area is wider than
  assumed). Not verified in game; the user's pack is the check.
