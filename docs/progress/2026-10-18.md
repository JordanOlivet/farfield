## Shaped blocks (ADR-036, plan item E.10)

Next step 2 of 2026-10-17: fences, walls and panes were invisible in LODs
and stairs were full cubes.

- `MaterialClassifier.SHAPED` and `ShapeBoxes` (core): a block's outline
  boxes in sixteenths, at most 8, kept per block state in `MaterialTable`
  (`Resolver.boxes`). `BlockKeys` classes as shaped the non-full blocks
  that cover their footprint without standing on the bottom (stairs, top
  slabs) and those with a collision shape (fences, walls, panes, bars,
  doors); blocks without collision (plants, torches, rails) stay out.
- `BinaryGreedyMesher` meshes each box of a shaped voxel as unit quads,
  box index in the fill bits; faces on the voxel side are culled by full
  opaque neighbors and lit by the voxel in front, inner faces by the
  shaped voxel. Coarse levels treat shaped blocks as opaque (`LodRenderer`).
- `MaterialColors` entries grow to 27 ints with the boxes; `lod.vert`
  moves the corners to the box; `lod.frag` discards textured texels below
  half coverage in the opaque pass (pane and bar frames, vanilla cutout).
- Tests: `shapedVoxelsMeshTheirBoxes`, `MaterialTableTest` (2); core 93
  tests green; `gradlew build` green.

## Generation spike S6 (E.11, ADR-037 proposed)

The user: "continue sur le seed gen" (next step 4 of 2026-10-17).

- `GenerationSpike` (common, server), `/farfield spike generation [side]`:
  noise preview timings (three ways to sample a column on a pool of 8
  threads, plus the accuracy of the cheap one), then square areas of fresh
  chunks generated up to noise, surface and full with Farfield's own
  region tickets, timed until all are done, with the process CPU.
- NeoForge dev server, three server runs (the last one ran the spike at 16
  then 32 chunks per side in one session); numbers in ADR-037. Stable
  across runs: `getBaseHeight` 2.3 ms per column and thread, preliminary
  surface 0.25 ms (12.5 blocks low on average), full chunks 50 to 75/s on
  about 5 cores, noise or surface 33 to 51/s on about 1.2 cores.
