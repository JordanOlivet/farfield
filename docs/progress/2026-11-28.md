### Done

- **ADR-180**: floating ground of two layers is not joined. Two slabs more
  than a sample's spacing apart in height are two layers
  (`PreviewTile.layerGap`). Between samples a place takes the layer of the
  nearest corner (`PreviewTile.floatCorners`), and a voxel column is the
  layer of its highest point (`PreviewVoxels.columnHeight`). No tile
  format change, nothing to rebuild.
- Four tests in `PreviewTest`: a block between two layers belongs to one
  island, no voxel fills the gap (1 and 4 blocks a voxel), a coarse tile
  cut into views keeps the layers apart, a thin slope stays one piece.
- ci-workflows, pull request #14, merged: `publish-mod.yml` passes
  `modrinth-unfeature-mode: none` to mc-publish (its v3.3.1 uploader
  returns at once on `NONE`, read in its source). The tag `v1` (v1.0.18,
  `1173387`) carries it; `actionlint` green.
