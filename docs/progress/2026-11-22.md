## The ice the user saw on the same lake

The user, in the same client: dashed pale strips on the frozen lake, and a
straight line across the lake ("la ligne droite qui traverse le lac. La teinte
de la glace LOD au delà est ok. Donc c'est l'ADR 55 qui a été cassé ou un truc
lié"). Four minimized NeoForge 1.21.1 runs over the lake by (48, 64), no Cirrus,
no API consumer (the branch then draws as `main`), and one with Rehearsal's own
client, without Farfield.

- Dashed pale strips: in the vanilla area, still there with `render off`, and
  there without Farfield too (same place, from (48, 110, 64) facing west, pitch
  30): the game's own, not ours.
- The straight line: drawn by the LODs (gone with `render off`), on level 0,
  from (48, 100, 40) facing yaw 20, pitch 15. A thinner pale band also runs
  along the vanilla edge itself (ADR-055's, left).
- The LOD ice's tint past both is close to vanilla's (3 RGB levels lighter on
  one view), as ADR-055 left it.
- The user, for the runs until the cause: "lance tant que tu n'as pas trouvé
  la cause, tu peux en lancer plusieurs". Steps (ADR-175 has the detail):
  a store probe (ice at y 62 over water, uniform; real data to z 239), the
  real sections meshed alone (no wall), preview on/off at the same view (the
  real ice ends at the line), a column report of the drawn sections (no
  overlap), the line's position with the spectator's flying field of view
  (77 degrees, not 70): z = 240, the real/preview border. Cause: the
  preview's ice showed its face against the preview's fluid mask (ADR-055's
  own-kind rule), so an ice wall stood along the border. Unit test first
  (failed), then the fix (passes), then the same two views in a dev client:
  the line gone, nothing else changed in the LODs.
- Temporary probes (a store probe task, a renderer column report) removed;
  `git status` held only the fix before the commit.

- The user, in a visible dev client with the fix over the same lake: "C'est
  nikel". The user kept the fix on this branch ("pas besoin de le sortir").
