## Band at the vanilla edge over sea ice (ADR-055)

The user, Fabric without Sodium (Sodium had been in the client's mods
folder during the 2026-10-21 check): "les bandes apparaissent quand un
chunk passe de LOD à vanilla [...] une bande de 1 blocs qui restait en LOD
[...] les LOD ont une luminosité différente de vanilla". With
`/farfield debug levels` the band stayed uncolored ("c'est pas des LOD");
F3+Q (F3+A on his AZERTY layout) made it vanish.

- Ruled out first: vanilla compiling a chunk before its neighbors' light
  (it re-meshes on every light change, `setSectionDirtyWithNeighbors`),
  stored light (14 in ice and 13 under it, border columns as interior).
- Cause: LOD ice was one translucent layer, vanilla's two (ice top and the
  water top under it); rays crossing from the last vanilla column into LOD
  ground missed the water layer. The mesher now keeps the faces between
  fluid and other translucent voxels.
- That exposed dark one-block lines along LOD section borders: a neighbor
  section's water drawn after the ice. Translucent sections are now drawn
  far to near.
