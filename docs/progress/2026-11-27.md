### The End's preview against real chunks (no code change)

The user's question: the far islands (preview) stand as tall walls, the
near ones (real) are flat. Measured with a temporary core test (deleted
after), which reads the real chunks of region files and the stored preview
tiles column by column.

- His pack holds Nullscape (`Nullscape_1.21.x_v1.2.14.jar`), which replaces
  the End's terrain.
- His session's save: real chunks reach about 780 blocks from the origin,
  and their ground beyond 600 blocks tops at 27 to 60 (p1 to p99). The
  preview's stored samples say the same out to 1,000 blocks (level 0, 600
  to 1,000 blocks: p99 59, max 62) and hold tall ground beyond (level 2,
  1,500 to 2,500 blocks: p50 55, p90 184, p99 256).
- Truth for the far band: a NeoForge dev server with Nullscape alone, a
  fresh world of the test seed, End chunks kept with `forceload` over four
  squares of 240 to 256 blocks (two the pack's preview stores as flat, two
  as tall), and the preview built over them. On 195,608 real ground
  columns, the real tops are 17/37/81/200/232 (p1/p10/p50/p90/p99).
- Preview against real on the same columns, the dev server's level-0
  tiles: tops 18/36/73/202/229 against 18/36/70/202/229, mean absolute
  difference 3.7 blocks; thickness 19.1 against 18.6 blocks. The tiles of
  his own pack session (same seed), level 2: tops 20/37/72/199/228 against
  17/36/69/202/229, mean absolute difference 6.4.
- So the tall ground is Nullscape's: beyond about 1,000 blocks its islands
  rise to y 200 and more, and the tiles hold the right top per column. The
  captures below show what the column figures hide: the walls between
  stacked islands.
- Seen on the way, both the other way round from his report: coarse tiles
  lower the tall ground (level 5: p90 165 against 200; level 6: mean top
  51.1 against 90.3), and they spread ground over the void beside the
  islands, thin (columns with preview ground and no real ground, against
  columns with both: 6,147 against 41,311 at level 0, 19,351 against
  43,161 at level 3, 32,919 against 44,073 at level 4; mean thickness 6 to
  11 blocks).

The dev server is back as it was (`level-name=world`, the spawn radius at
4096, no jar in `mods`, the test world deleted).

### The same views in a client: the preview walls up stacked islands

The user, on the figures above: "Oui fais les tests." One NeoForge 1.21.1
dev client, minimized, at 3840x2019, Nullscape in the run's mods, a fresh
`farfield-test` (the test seed), `end-ab.cmd` through Rehearsal (1,153 s):
two views of the End's zone x 1280 to 2047, z 0 to 767, first as preview,
then after `forceload` made its 2,304 chunks real (`LOD pipeline (server)
minecraft:the_end: 2704 snapshots in the last minute`), then with the
preview off, which leaves the real LODs alone.

- From 700, 170, 384 facing east, and from 0, 197, 0 as in his captures:
  where the real terrain is small islands stacked in layers with the void
  between them, the preview draws solid towers and curtains from the top
  island down to the lower one. With the level colors they are 4-block
  voxels (yellow) at the near view, 8-block (green) at the far one.
- The tile holds one top and one underside per sample, right at each
  sample (the figures above). Read in the code, not yet proven by a test:
  the walls come from joining two samples of different layers.
  `PreviewTile.floatCorners` sets apart only the samples without ground;
  two samples with ground are interpolated, whatever the gap between
  their slabs. `PreviewVoxels.columnHeight` gives a voxel column the
  highest top and the lowest underside of its footprint's samples, so a
  footprint over both layers is solid from one to the other.
- So his report holds: the heights are real, the walls are not. My first
  reading of the figures ("the relief is real") was half of it.
- Captures: `neoforge/run/rehearsal/shots/v1-*.png` and `v2-*.png` (ten,
  gitignored), and the crops `end-ab-v2-centre.png`,
  `end-ab-v2-centre-levels.png`, `end-ab-v1-centre.png` beside them
  (preview, real with the preview around, real alone).

The dev client is back as it was (`options.txt` and the client settings
from their `.orig`, no jar in `mods`, `saves/farfield-test` deleted: the
