## Done

- The user asked whether ADR-118 changed performance. Tile builds did not
  move (2.9 to 4.5 s per tile before and after, noise from the spot), but
  the client's preview mesh jobs had become 3 to 10 times slower: the
  pack logs of 2026-11-07 at -925, 120, 1390 read 1.3 to 3 ms per job before
  ADR-118 and 6 to 26 ms after, 35 ms on a full remesh (the preview light
  switch) against 2.8 to 5.2 ms before.
- Cause: `PreviewVoxels.dig` read the lakes through the raw tile source,
  not the section's tile cache. On the client that source is
  `PreviewStore.read`, a shared lock and an LRU lookup per call, and it ran
  1 to 4 times per column on the finest level, up to 64 times on coarser
  ones (16 samples, up to 4 tiles each), for every tile holding a lake (most
  of them).
- Fix: `PreviewVoxels.NearTiles`, the tiles of the section's area (its
  columns, one voxel beside, the features' margin), each looked up once on
  first use; `dig` reads through it. Regression test
  `PreviewTest.aSectionLooksUpItsTilesAFewTimesNotOncePerColumn`: 88 to 233
  source reads per section on levels 0 to 4 with the fix, 2,685 on level 0
  without it (the test fails then, checked by swapping the argument back).
