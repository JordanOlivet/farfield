### Done

- **The Nether: as decided.** With the thick fog distance at its default,
  vanilla, the LODs take the game's fog and nothing is selected beyond it
  (ADR-033, ADR-034, the user's choice of the Nether's haze). The setting's
  tooltip says "little of it shows"; the count says none. Unchanged.
- **The End: a side effect, fixed (ADR-179).** The dragon's boss bar brings
  the same thick fog within 192 blocks of the End's centre while the dragon
  lives (read in the game's source, 1.20.1 to 26.3), and the fog mixin took
  it for a thick dimension: no LOD from the main island of a new world.
  `BossHealthOverlayMixin` answers the game's question itself: while LODs
  are drawn, a boss bar creates no world fog, so the fog, the sky and the
  End flash's light are the dimension's own. One class for every version,
  registered in the six mixin configs.
- **A scenario for it**: `dev/rehearsal/scenarios/dimension-fog.cmd` (End
  under the boss bar, End out of its range, Nether, each at vanilla and
  off), with the counts to read.

### Verified

NeoForge dev clients, minimized, the test world with its dragon alive; the
counts are LOD sections drawn, read from the scenario's `state` replies.

- The capture under the boss bar at vanilla: a black frame before; after,
  the far islands, the End's sky and the dragon, the boss bar still shown.
- `Boss fog left out while the LODs are drawn (ADR-179)` once in each
  game log after the fix; no `Mixin apply failed`, no crash report.
- `gradlew build --no-daemon` green: the twelve jars hold the mixin and its
  config line; Forge 1.20.1's refmap names the target.
- The run dirs' test saves and client settings were put back from copies
  after each run.
