## Done

### Texture colors and biome tints (`779c9f0`)

- `MaterialColors`: per block state and face, the alpha-weighted average
  of the model's sprites in linear space, layered in drawing order (the
  grass overlay over the grass side), as an untinted part plus a
  biome-tinted part: `color = untinted + tinted * tint`. Constant block
  colors (birch, spruce) are baked in; fluids and models without quads use
  their particle sprite; the map color is only a fallback. Waterlogged
  partial blocks, seagrass and kelp take their fluid's look
  (`BlockKeys.lookOf`, mirrors `BlockKeys.resolve`). Built on the render
  thread under 3 ms per frame.
- `BiomeTints`: one tint kind per `ColorResolver` a block color asks for
  (grass, foliage, water, then mods', up to 8), found by calling
  `BlockColors` with `TintProbe`, a one-block level that records the
  resolver; a table per biome of the LOD world and kind, uploaded as a
  second storage buffer.
- Quads carry the biome id + 1 in bits 48-59 for materials whose color
  depends on the biome; `BinaryGreedyMesher` splits those faces by 4x4x4
  biome cell (only when the section's cells differ). Two new unit tests,
  one of them against brute force on random biomes.
- Colors are rebuilt on resource reload (missing model changes) and on
  level change, and resident meshes are meshed again. Before, the color
  table was never reset when the LOD world changed (another dimension got
  the previous one's colors).
- `SpriteContentsAccessor` reads the sprite pixels (`originalImage`);
  present in both jars' refmap.

### Translucent water (`5c93273`)

- Each section's quads are stored opaque first, then translucent; two
  indirect draws, the second blended with the face's texture coverage and
  without depth writes (the `first` of the draw command offsets
  `gl_VertexID` into the translucent quads).
- The mesher takes optional solid border layers of the neighbors, used
  only to cull translucent faces: water spanning sections shows no inner
  walls, opaque borders stay closed (no cracks between levels). Sections
  with translucent voxels read their six same-level neighbors; a rewritten
  section meshes its translucent neighbors again. Unit tests extended
  (brute force with solid neighbors, water spanning sections).

### Hand-off fog (`5a2470a`)

- `FogRendererMixin` (optional): while LODs are drawn, vanilla's normal
  terrain fog takes the LOD fog distances (2,458 to 4,096 blocks). Left
  alone: water, lava, powder snow, mob-effect fog, thick dimension fog and
  fog a mod changed (detected as fog end != far plane).

### Leaves

Not changed: with texture colors and the foliage tint, LOD forests look
like the vanilla ones in the dev client. Left for the user's check in the
real install, where the remark was made (with map colors).

## Screen-space detail and vertical hand-off

- `lod.frag` discards only within the vanilla radius horizontally and
  vertically. Checked in the Fabric dev client with the pack's Sodium jar
  in `fabric/runs/client/mods` (gitignored): at y=245 above the lake the
  ground is continuous; with `/farfield render off` at the same spot the
  frame is only sky (Sodium draws nothing below), so the LODs fill it.
- `LodRenderer.updateDetail`: `LodSelector` detail from the focal length
  and `RenderSettings.voxelPixels()` (`RenderToggle` renamed
  `RenderSettings`), `/farfield detail [px]`, the detail in the stat line.
  The first frame's projection can be degenerate (m11 infinite, seen on
  Fabric): ignored, else every section splits to level 0 (138,000
  resident, 756 MB on the first try).
- Measurements and the default (4 px): ADR-023.

## Textures on the LOD faces (ADR-025)

- `FaceTextures`: 2048-wide sRGB atlas of 16x16 tiles, five mip levels
  built per tile (alpha-weighted box filter in linear space), nearest
  filtering, deduplicated tiles, CPU copy of every level (grows by
  doubling its height).
- `MaterialColors` rewritten: each face is baked by projecting the model's
  quads onto a 16x16 grid in the face's U and V axes (vertex positions and
  UVs), sprite frame 0, box filter for textures above 16 px; untinted and
  tinted tiles; holes of opaque materials filled at 45 % brightness; the
  mean colors come from the tiles. Stride 18 ints per state (three per
  face: untinted color, tinted color, tiles).
- Shaders: the color moves to the fragment shader; `textureGrad` with the
  derivatives of the unwrapped block coordinates (no mip seams at block
  edges); fade to the mean color over the last quarter of
  `RenderSettings.textureDistance()`; `/farfield textures <blocks>`.
- Checked in the Fabric dev client with Sodium at render distance 2 (LODs
  from about 16 blocks): leaves read as vanilla fast leaves, bark, sand,
  grass and snow textured, matching the vanilla trees next to them; 2,066
  tiles (atlas 2048x512). GPU cost in ADR-025 (noise level).
- Found on the way: Sodium redirects `glfwCreateWindow` like the old
  `WindowMixin`; when Mixin skipped ours the game ran on OpenGL 3.2 and
  LODs were disabled (one dev run out of five so far; the user's pack had
  been lucky). Fixed with an injection and a hidden 4.6 probe window
  (`45098eb`).
