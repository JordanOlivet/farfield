## Second test of the day (ADR-141)

The user on the Fabric pack with ADR-140: "il y a toujours les saccades en
avançant, photon gcea est toujours cassé avec les carrés noirs et si on
regarde la preview de super loin en hauteur, on a comme une texture grise
qui se superpose"; the stutters while flying in creative with sprint.

- Photon: black past about 800,000 blocks, from its own combined depth
  (game near plane 0.05 to the LOD extent); the LODs now stop at 262,144
  blocks under a shader pack. Checked in the Fabric pack at 4K with the
  distance at its maximum, day and night.
- The grey cast from far up: the near plane now follows the nearest drawn
  section; checked from 2,200,000 up.
- The stutters: young G1 pauses (78 a minute, 15 to 34 ms) from about 4 GB
  allocated a second while flying. Two allocation fixes (far tile lookups
  memoized, the preview store's maps under spread keys) shortened the
  pauses (17.2 to 15.2 ms on average) but not their count. With
  `-XX:+UseZGC -XX:+ZGenerational` the pauses are 0.023 ms at most.
- A crash found and fixed in this round before any commit: a static array
  read by the renderer's constructor before the class had set it.
- The stat line now names the longest LOD frame's steps.

## Less allocation (ADR-142)

"Fais la baisse de l'allocation dans la foulée". Thread scratch arrays
for the preview build and the tree stamps, the masks' keys spread, the far
tile memo reused, `LodSection` work arrays made lazily, the meshers'
section cache at 4,096. Same flight, same conditions (minimized, G1):
Farfield's garbage 88 to about 61 GB a minute, young pauses 14.8 to about
12.5 ms, over 15 ms 34 to about 8, their count unchanged (the game's and
the other mods' garbage). The client was run minimized while the user used
the PC.
