## Face directions (E.12)

Before: one indirect command per section and pass with all its quads; the
rasterizer culled back faces after the vertex shader had run on them.

Now each section's quads are stored in 12 groups (opaque by direction,
then translucent by direction; `Resident.groups`), and the draw loop emits
one command per run of adjacent groups whose direction can face the camera
(`FaceDirections.front`, core: +X faces only when the camera is past the
box's min X, with one block of slack). No shader change: `gl_VertexID`
starts at the command's `first`, as the translucent pass already relied on.
The stat line now says `N of M quads sent (face directions)`.
