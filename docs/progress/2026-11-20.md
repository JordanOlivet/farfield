## The 26.3 preview in the clients, and grey water on 26.x

The user: "tu peux lancer les tests". Minimized dev clients (Rehearsal), the test
world, a jump to (12000, 230, 12000), unseen terrain.

- Both loaders build and draw the preview: fill after joining 263 quarters in about
  12 s, after the jump 82 quarters in about 10 s; 0 features failed; relief, forests,
  sand, snow and lakes as the land around them.
- Found: the preview's rivers and lakes were grey, and so would be every LOD's water
  on 26.1 and up. The heights were right (a tile dump: much of the tile under sea
  level 63, its water material set); the look was not. Since 26.1 a fluid's biome
  color is its `FluidModel`'s tint source; the water block's own in `BlockColors`
  is the particles' (white in the world), so the look baked the water sprite
  untinted. `MaterialColors` now takes a model-less fluid block's tint from its fluid
  model (26.1 on). The looks cache (`looks.bin`) kept the grey look: `BAKE_VERSION`
  2 bakes every look again once.
- Verified: a temporary log in the rebuilt look, `tintKind=2` (the water resolver) and
  a blue tint, then captures of blue rivers and lakes, Fabric then NeoForge 26.3.
- Not verified: water on 26.1.2 and 26.2 in a client (same code path, not run);
  whether the NeoForge 26.3 window still flashes at start.

## The remaining checks, and two more bugs

The user: "pousse et fais les test qu'il reste". `feat/26x` pushed (466816f).

- Clients, water and preview at (12000, 12000), minimized: Fabric and NeoForge
  26.2 and 26.1.2. Blue water everywhere, the same image as 26.3, 0 features
  failed, about 12 s to fill after the jump. Only the dev login's 401 in the logs.
- Found: on 26.2 the preview's surface rules never ran column by column (`context
  updates true false`): 26.2's `updateY` takes four ints (`blockY` only, X and Z
  from `updateXZ`). The preview fell back to the stand-in chunks and guessed the
  coarse tiles' ground. Fixed in `SurfaceColumns`; seen: `the surface rules run
  column by column` on the 26.2 dev server.
- Found: a pregeneration on an empty dedicated server stopped a minute in on
  1.21.11 and 26.x (55 of 64 level-0 tiles on 26.2, then nothing; 26.1.2's thread
  dump: the five preview workers idle). The game pauses an empty server after
  `pause-when-empty-seconds` (60), and the preview's planner runs on the server's
  ticks. ADR-172: while a task runs, `DedicatedServerMixin` keeps the server
  ticking. Seen on Fabric 26.1.2: the spawn task (368 tiles) done in 1.9 min and
  the task at (20000, -20000) after it, the minute lines printed, then `no task
  left, the server pauses when empty again` and the game's own pause a minute
  later; Fabric 1.21.11 the same.
- Tile timing, Fabric dev server, 109 tiles within 2,048 blocks of (20000,
  -20000), preview store emptied first: 26.2 0.5 min, 26.3 0.5 min, 1.21.11
