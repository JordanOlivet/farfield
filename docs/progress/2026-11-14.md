## The join as a wave (study)

The user on ADR-154 (Fabric pack): fewer holes, but the view comes in
phases (near ground without trees, near trees alone for a while, then
everything at once): "j'ai comme une impression qu'on tourne encore en
rond". What he wants: the world unrolling from the player as a wave, the
near terrain fine fairly soon, the rest refining farther and farther.
Asked to look at what the field does; "go étude".

`docs/analysis/join-as-a-wave.md`: a temporary probe counted the mesh
jobs by cause on one run (Fabric pack, new world, largest distance, 12
chunks), removed after (jars rebuilt, same hash as the deployed one):
- 78,643 jobs in 50 s for 32,683 sections; 22 % of the meshes never
  drawn;
- preview sections meshed again: 106 jobs in 30 s, 35,515 by 50 s (held
  behind every hole to the border, ADR-153's lag): the burst;
- 63 % of the first 20 s' jobs are the spawn's real sections, 4.2 meshes
  each;
- the near detail waits for the tiles: within 1,024 blocks built at 56 s.

Proposal, nothing built: one score for meshes and tiles (distance, times
a factor for a refinement) in place of the steps and lags; fewer meshes
(once per tile for the preview, the spawn's real sections at rest); the
disc kept as a safety; the probe kept as a performance log line.

Not verified: the proposal itself; the two leads on the real sections
are to check in the code.
