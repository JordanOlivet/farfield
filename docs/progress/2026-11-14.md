## Four steps (ADR-152)

The user on ADR-151: the coarse preview to the border past an empty ring
beyond the first 1,024 blocks; proposed four steps and a smaller ring;
"go 4 temps avec 512 pour commencer". Also found: the meshers were busy
all the time, each finer tile near the player meshing the same sections
again while the middle distances waited.

- The preview's steps: the coarsest level, the near ring (view distance
  plus 512 blocks) at an acceptable then the wanted level, then every
  distance at an acceptable then the wanted level; the levels between not
  built for players; by each player's own detail. Clients ask in the same
  steps.
- A drawn section's new mesh waits behind the holes within 16,384 blocks.

instead of 45, 27 % fewer meshes, land to the horizon at 5 s; the near
