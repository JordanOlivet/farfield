## What was done

The user: "vas y et fais aussi B.2 et B.3 à la suite" (streaming to finish
before 0.2.0, change notices first; the user tests by hand tonight or
tomorrow, these are dev-run checks).

- **Change notices (protocol 4).** New message `CHANGED` (session, regions
  as `LodKey.pack(level, rx, 0, rz)`). `StreamServer` listens to the
  section writes of each streamed world; `RegionChanges` keeps the
  written regions and hands one over once it has been quiet for 3 s, or
  20 s after its first write not announced yet (an import writing a
  region for minutes still reaches the clients, and an index is not read
  again more than every 20 s per region). Once a second the server tells
  each player the due regions within its radius. The client asks those
  indexes again (only regions it knows or asked for); the renderer's
  reselection then fetches the sections whose version moved, the stale
  copies drawn meanwhile. Fixes the case of 2026-10-25: a client joined
  during the server's import kept the sections from before it.
- **Fetch window (AIMD).** `FetchWindow`: the client's sections in flight,
  64 at most, 4 at least, one more per window of timely answers, halved
  when an answer comes back 1 s later than the quickest one (once per
  round trip), back to 4 on a timeout. Starts at 64, so a good link
  fills as fast as before. Its size is on the client's stat line
  (`in flight of N`).
- **Server stat line:** highest ping of the streamed players and the
  longest queue of messages.
- Core tests: `CHANGED` round trip, `RegionChanges` (quiet time, longest
  wait), `FetchWindow` (halving once per round trip, growth, timeout).
