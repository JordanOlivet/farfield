## C.1: the shader packs' contracts and the C.1a spike

The user wants any shader pack to shade the LODs and the preview as it
shades vanilla terrain, through the Distant Horizons and Voxy formats,
ideally with no pack-side setup. Their answers: Voxy first, then DH;
packs with neither contract, our own contract and LOD shadows later.
Both contracts are in `docs/analysis/shader-lod-contracts.md` (DH's from
Iris's LGPL source; Voxy's from the packs only, Voxy being ARR, ADR-001),
the decision in ADR-113, the split in C.1a to C.1e.

The spike (C.1a, `photon-gcea` in the pack client, Iris 1.8.14-beta.1,
NeoForge; the run dir's `config/iris.properties` set to
`enableShaders=true`, the original in `iris.properties.orig`):

- `farfield.iris.mixins.json`, applied only when Iris is present and
  Voxy absent: `VOXY` among Iris's standard macros, the three Voxy files
  read as program starts, `vxDepthTexOpaque`/`vxDepthTexTrans` added to
  the render-target samplers, the `vx*` matrices and `vxRenderDistance`
  added to the common uniforms.
- `IrisLink` (behind `ShaderPackLink`, loaded by reflection): reads the
  pack's `voxy.json` per dimension (tolerant per-key parse: Photon
  includes its settings into a string field), builds a unit of the
  pack's `voxy_opaque.glsl` with the uniforms and samplers it lists,
  links it into `lod.frag`'s `VOXY` variant, binds Iris's uniforms and
  samplers to those programs, and draws the opaque and plant passes into
  a gbuffer framebuffer of the pack's draw buffers with a `DEPTH32F`
  depth of the LODs' own, cleared each frame at `renderLevel` HEAD. The
  translucent pass is skipped under a pack until C.1b.
- Runs 1 to 6 showed no LODs. Run 1 was black (the pack read the LOD
  depth and matrices before they existed). Runs 2 to 6: every LOD draw
  failed with `GL_INVALID_FRAMEBUFFER_OPERATION` (GPU about 6 us for
  300k quads): Iris's gbuffer framebuffer carries the game's
  depth-stencil texture, and our depth replaced only its depth half;
  status `0x8CDD` (unsupported), read as complete at first. Run 5 was
  stopped by Claude Code on low system memory (1.8 GB free); the
  orphaned 14.7 GB client was closed and the run started again on the
  user's "relance".
- Fix: `GL_DEPTH_STENCIL_ATTACHMENT` detached before our depth goes on.
  Run 7: status `8cd5`, 311,673 of 409,920 LOD depth texels written, no
  GL error after any pass, GPU 485 to 878 us per frame, and the
  captures show the LODs lit by Photon (`render off` leaves sky only).

Verified: `gradlew build` passes; run 7 in the pack client as above.
Not verified: the user's eye, a night shot, Fabric (compiled against
Iris 1.8.8, never run), shaders off in the same build (the `VANILLA`
path is unchanged code, not run since), translucent LODs (not drawn
under a pack yet).
