## The quad buffer shrinks back after a zoom (ADR-120)

- The user picked it from the remaining work ("go buffer"): the buffer
  doubled when full (64 MB first, 1 GB at most) and never shrank; a
  spyglass zoom in their pack on 2026-11-08 left it at 256 MB with 32 MB
  used.
- `LodRenderer.shrinkArena`, every 60 frames after the unused sections are
  evicted: a buffer at most a quarter used for 10 s is compacted into the
  smallest 64 MB times a power of two holding twice the use
  (`RangeAllocator.shrunkCapacity`, unit-tested), the residents' quads
  copied on the GPU and each section's record rewritten. One `info` line.
- Agent runs, Fabric pack in production, no shader pack, 854x480, the
  test world at -1264, 120, 882:
  1. `/farfield debug level 0`: no growth (a level lock is a floor, level
     0 changes nothing); 8 MB of 64 used.
  2. `/farfield detail 1` then 6: 54 MB of 64 at the finest detail, so no
     growth at this window size either; 7 MB used after.
  3. The same with a temporary first size of 32 MB (reverted, not
     committed): `grown to 64 MB` at the finest detail, then 16 s after
     detail 6 freed 4,148 meshes, `shrunk from 64 to 32 MB (7 MB used,
     538 sections moved)`. The view before the growth and after the
     compaction at the same spot match (mean difference 1.8 of 765, 0.4 %
     of pixels above 60, the scene's noise); a teleport after it drew the
     sea and coast as usual, the buffer at 6 MB of 32.

- ADR-120 in the user's pack: not tested, the user's call ("c'est mineur,
  je te fais confiance sur ce point").
