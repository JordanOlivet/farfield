## Step 10: the client/server run

Fabric dev server (fresh world, view 12, the pack server's streaming
settings) and Fabric dev client at 4K, a join then six jumps east, JFR on
both (attached with `jcmd`: a `JAVA_TOOL_OPTIONS` recording broke Gradle's
toolchain search). Figures and leads in the review document, "Step 10".

- The stream runs at its `player_kbps` budget in flight (10,240 KB per
  10 s, sections pushed 1.3 to 2 s after their write): the budget sets the
  pace, not the processor.
- 83 % of the server workers' time is the coarse levels built again
  (`LodWorld.buildParent`), not the ingest (17 %).
- The user: "go S1 S2 S3". S3 (the stream's surface floors kept per column)
  built: stream processor 6.7 s a minute to 1.6 to 2.0 s. S2 (sections just
  written kept decoded for their parents) built: children read back 12.1 %
  to 7.6 % of the workers' samples. S1 (coarse levels paced) built,
  measured with temporary counters, and removed: 15 % fewer parents built,
  no gain in processor time beyond the spread between runs.
- Unit tests: parents identical byte for byte with kept and read-back
  children; kept floors give the same hidden sections and read only the
  changed column. `gradlew build` passes; no `TEMP` left. Not committed.
- Then "go 1" (2026-11-16): a parent downsamples only the children written
  since its last build and copies its other octants from its stored copy
  (measured first: 2.2 of 8 children changed on average). Parents identical
  byte for byte with whole builds (unit test). Server workers 7.0 to 7.9 ms
  a chunk in flight before, 5.1 ms after. Committed (10856ff) on "Commit
  tout", without the skill notes autoharness changed (they name the home
  server's host and paths).
- Then the kept octants copied brick by brick instead of voxel by voxel:
  parents still identical byte for byte (the test, with palettes that grow
  while copied, fails on a deliberate error in the copy); workers 5.1 to
  4.6 ms a chunk in flight. Not committed.
- Then "Mesure tout ce qui pourrait permettre de gagner des perfs": two
  probe runs (counters removed since, `gradlew build` passes) and the hot
  lines of both profiles. Seven leads, all identical in image, in the review
  document ("Measured leads"): the largest is the client's real-ground scan
  beside the preview (95.5 % of its reads in air bricks, each chunk scanned
  about 23 times in 10 s, 11.9 % of the mesher), then `LodSection.pack`'s
  division, the downsampling's read into the brick above, uniform chunk
  sections walked voxel by voxel. Nothing built.
- Then "go L1 L2 L3 L4 L6 et étudie L5 et L7": L1 to L4 built, L6 reduced
  to a last-material memory in `LodSection.set`; a golden hash of the
  downsampling and a reference for the real ground compare with the old

  source only, no test: Minecraft code). Server workers 4.6 to
  3.9 ms a chunk in flight, the mesher 1,119 to 996 us of processor a job.
