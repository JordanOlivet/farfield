## The holes in new terrain, again: two causes on the client

The user flew over new terrain: "ça arrive bien moins souvent mais ça
arrive toujours", with the feeling that the server-to-client model cannot
give a smooth experience. 11 `debug here` reports (client log of the
instance above): 0 wanted, 0 in flight every time, so not the transport.
Two reports show a hole where the preview is hidden for real terrain and
a finer level draws nothing: a level-0 section drawn with 0 quads at
y 64-95 where level 4 puts the ground at 80; the view through the ground
down to y 61 under a drawn level-1 section.

Reading the selector and the stream (ADR-074):

1. `LodSelector` kept a split parent split when a child was not ready,
   on the grounds that a new child had nothing drawn before it. On a
   mirror the coarse levels arrive first (level 4 is fetched in the
   background for the preview's mask), so the parent did show that area:
   a hole until the child was fetched and meshed.
2. A drawn copy older than the server's waited behind the whole
   background queue, while the preview already hid itself for the newer
   level 4.

First proposal to the user was a completeness flag per section in the
index; dropped: the preview hides itself chunk by chunk from level 4, so
a section missing chunks is already covered there. No protocol change.

- **Fix:** a mesh records the octants its quads draw in
  (`Quad.octants`, `Resident.octants`); a split parent keeps splitting
  over a child that is not ready only if it draws nothing in that
  child's octant, otherwise it draws itself until the child is meshed.
- **Fix:** sections drawn from an outdated copy are marked stale at
  selection, so they are fetched with the selection's wishes.
- **Measures:** server stat line: written regions announced N ms after
  their first write on average and at most; client stream line: outdated
  sections refreshed N ms after their index, and how many wait; renderer
  line: outdated sections drawn per selection.
- Tests: `LodSelectorTest.descendingParentFallsBackWhenItShowsWhatANewChildLacks`,
  `QuadTest`, `RegionChangesTest`.

## The server pushes what it writes (ADR-075)

- `StreamServer`: the store listener queues each written section for the
  players whose client draws its level there (`pushRange`, from the
  client's `HINT` detail; level 4 in the whole radius); a reader task
  turns up to 32 queued keys into `SECTION` messages whenever a player's
  queue holds fewer than 64 messages. A record cache per world (512) is
  shared by the players. Pushed level-0 sections pass the surface rule
  per section (`SurfaceIndex.hidden(key, floors)`, column floors cached,
  dropped on write).
- `LodPipeline`: sections written once untouched for 250 ms, maintenance
  every 250 ms (was 3 s and 1 s).
- `StreamClient`: sends `HINT` (the renderer's detail) at the session's
  start and on a 5 % move; a refreshed coarse index asks the finer
  region's index again (`finer indexes asked again`); counts pushed
  sections.
- Protocol 5. Older clients are declined and build their own LODs.

Verified: `gradlew build`, core tests (`StreamProtocolTest` 10,
`SurfaceIndexTest` 8, `LodSelectorTest` 6). Not verified: no dev run, not
in the pack; the push delay, the bandwidth at the generation front and
the surface rule on pushes are unchecked in game.

  a section not meshed yet draws its ready children meanwhile
  (`LodSelector`, `finer fallbacks`).

## ADR-076 in the pack; a hole detector and less meshing (ADR-077)

Deployed (`370e634`, sha256 `745a6c09…b609`). The user: "ça arrive
toujours". 11 `debug here` reports came after the holes were filled
(every section held and current). The mesher ran at its limit: up to
31,444 meshes per 10 s on 4 threads, 64 in flight, for 2,000 to 4,800
sections received; `finer fallbacks` up to 6, coarse up to 97.

- Hole detector: meshes record the chunk columns their quads draw in;
  every 250 ms the renderer counts the columns within 32 chunks, beyond
  the game's render distance, where nothing drawn has a quad, times them,
  and logs the first three new ones per 10 s level by level
  (`LOD hole at chunk`, `LOD holes:` line).
- Held preview masks wait for real quads in each new chunk column.
- Neighbors are meshed again only when a section's border layer changed
  (hash per direction) or it had no data before; a changed preview mask
  meshes only the preview sections over the chunks it changed.
- Auto mesh and preview threads: one per three logical processors, 1 to
  6 (the user's 16: 5, was 4).

Verified: `gradlew build`, `QuadTest` 2, `RenderConfigTest` 4,
`LodSelectorTest` 7. Not verified in game.
