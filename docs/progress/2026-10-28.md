## The holes in new terrain, again: two causes on the client

The user flew over new terrain: "ça arrive bien moins souvent mais ça
arrive toujours", with the feeling that the server-to-client model cannot
give a smooth experience. 11 `debug here` reports (client log of the
instance above): 0 wanted, 0 in flight every time, so not the transport.
Two reports show a hole where the preview is hidden for real terrain and
a finer level draws nothing: a level-0 section drawn with 0 quads at
y 64-95 where level 4 puts the ground at 80; the view through the ground
down to y 61 under a drawn level-1 section.

Reading the selector and the stream (ADR-074):

1. `LodSelector` kept a split parent split when a child was not ready,
   on the grounds that a new child had nothing drawn before it. On a
   mirror the coarse levels arrive first (level 4 is fetched in the
   background for the preview's mask), so the parent did show that area:
   a hole until the child was fetched and meshed.
2. A drawn copy older than the server's waited behind the whole
   background queue, while the preview already hid itself for the newer
   level 4.

First proposal to the user was a completeness flag per section in the
index; dropped: the preview hides itself chunk by chunk from level 4, so
a section missing chunks is already covered there. No protocol change.

- **Fix:** a mesh records the octants its quads draw in
  (`Quad.octants`, `Resident.octants`); a split parent keeps splitting
  over a child that is not ready only if it draws nothing in that
  child's octant, otherwise it draws itself until the child is meshed.
- **Fix:** sections drawn from an outdated copy are marked stale at
  selection, so they are fetched with the selection's wishes.
- **Measures:** server stat line: written regions announced N ms after
  their first write on average and at most; client stream line: outdated
  sections refreshed N ms after their index, and how many wait; renderer
  line: outdated sections drawn per selection.
- Tests: `LodSelectorTest.descendingParentFallsBackWhenItShowsWhatANewChildLacks`,
  `QuadTest`, `RegionChangesTest`.
